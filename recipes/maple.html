🍁 Maple-Ginger Chicken & Rice Bowls

Based on your earlier chicken bowl; simplified demonstration quantities.

Servings: 3

Ingredients

  • 800 g Chicken breast
  • 278 g Dry white rice
  • 2 cups Water for rice
  • 28 g Verified-safe cooking oil
  • 6 tbsp Coconut aminos (check label)
  • 3 tbsp Pure maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp Brown sugar
  • 1.25 tsp Ground ginger
  • 1.375 tsp Onion powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1.5 tsp Cornstarch

Directions

  1. Cook rice according to its package directions.
  2. Season chicken with ginger, onion powder and paprika; cook in oil to 165°F.
  3. Simmer coconut aminos, maple syrup and brown sugar with a splash of water; thicken with cornstarch slurry.
  4. Toss chicken with sauce and serve over rice.
