🥣 Chicken Taco Soup

Demo version using single-ingredient spices; check canned food and stock labels.

Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb Chicken breast
  • 1 can Canned black beans (check label)
  • 1 can Canned diced tomatoes (check label)
  • 1 cup Frozen corn
  • 3 cups Water or verified-safe stock
  • 2 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Onion powder

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a pot; bring to a gentle simmer.
  2. Cook until chicken reaches 165°F, about 20–30 minutes depending on thickness.
  3. Remove and shred chicken, return to pot and simmer another 5 minutes.
  4. Taste and adjust seasoning.
