🍯 Honey-Lime Salmon & Rice

Demo adaptation of your honey-lime salmon meal.

Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 16 oz Skinless salmon
  • 0.75 cup Dry white rice
  • 2 tbsp Honey
  • 2 tbsp Fresh lime juice
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 2 cups Broccoli

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 400°F.
  2. Cook rice and steam broccoli.
  3. Mix honey, lime juice and olive oil; brush over salmon.
  4. Bake salmon until the thickest part reaches 145°F, then serve with rice and broccoli.
