🐷 Simple Pulled Pork

Garlic-free base recipe; no commercial BBQ sauce assumed safe.

Servings: 6

Ingredients

  • 3 lb Pork shoulder
  • 0.25 cup Apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbsp Brown sugar
  • 2 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp Onion powder
  • 1.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 cup Water

Directions

  1. Mix seasonings and rub over pork.
  2. Add pork, water and vinegar to a slow cooker.
  3. Cook on low about 8–10 hours until tender and easy to shred.
  4. Shred, discard bones and excess fat, and mix with cooking juices.
