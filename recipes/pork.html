🐷 BBQ Pulled Pork (3–5 lb)

For a 3–5 lb bone-in shoulder, with a full batch of homemade BBQ sauce. No garlic or Worcestershire is added; packaged ingredients still require allergy verification.

One batch: keep the rub, apple juice and full sauce quantities below for any 3–5 lb roast.

Allergy checks

Check every packaged ingredient for milk, soy, garlic, barley, watermelon and egg, including spices, flavorings, ketchup, molasses and vinegar. Use the ketchup only once its ingredients and any unclear flavorings are verified garlic-free and suitable for your allergies. UPC matching does not verify allergen safety.

Directions

  1. Season: Combine the dry rub ingredients and coat the pork evenly.
  2. Slow cook: Place the pork in the slow cooker and pour the apple juice around it. Cook on LOW until fork-tender: approximately 7–8 hours for 3 lb, 8–9 hours for 4 lb, or 9–10 hours for 5 lb. Start checking earlier; time varies by roast and cooker. The pork should pull apart effortlessly, typically around 195–205°F internally.
  3. Brown (optional): Transfer the pork to a roasting pan, reserving the slow-cooker juices. Spoon a little reserved pork fat over it and roast at 350°F for 20 minutes. Remove the thick fat cap and roast another 15 minutes.
  4. Make the sauce: Combine all homemade BBQ sauce ingredients and the Worcestershire-free flavor replacement in a saucepan. Simmer for approximately 45 minutes until thickened.
  5. Finish the sauce: Defat the reserved slow-cooker juices, then gradually add ½–⅔ cup to the sauce until it reaches your desired consistency.
  6. Shred: Remove bones and excess fat. Shred the pork with two forks, then gently toss with sauce to taste. Make the full sauce batch even for a 3 lb roast; refrigerate leftover sauce for up to 3–4 days or freeze.
